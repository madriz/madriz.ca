AI Commerce

ShopButler

An independent agentic shopping engine that works for the buyer, not the store. ShopButler finds the true landed price across every online retailer, ranks results with no merchant paying for placement, and completes the purchase as a declared, cryptographically signed agent. Built on Visa's Trusted Agent Protocol, Mastercard Agent Pay, Anthropic MCP, Google's UCP and AP2, and Cloudflare's Web Bot Auth. One engine serving three applications: white-label for banks, direct-to-consumer subscription, and enterprise procurement.

AI / Finance

ContextQuant

An AI-driven contextual intelligence platform that reads corporate disclosures as a connected system. Initially built on the Dow 30 and their direct competitors, it now covers the North American financial sector, pharmaceuticals, as well as the Nasdaq 100 plus their competitors. So far it has analyzed close to 100K SEC filings and 1B+ words, covering earnings calls, macro data, executive compensation, and political spending. Its primary application is for equity research & credit risk, providing early warning signals on bond yields, credit ratings, and related instruments. Expanding to additional North American and global markets.

HR Tech

Credentia One

This is an experiment to create a new transport layer for professional credentials. Traditional CVs are unstructured documents that applicant tracking systems (ATS) routinely fail to parse, filtering out qualified candidates for technical rather than merit-based reasons. Credentia.One replaces the document with a structured, machine-readable format (Credentia.JSON) and a two-part codec system for creation and interpretation/validation.

Consumer / Data

HomeGadgets

Seeks to be Canada's best price comparison site focused on over 20 of the largest categories within consumer electronics and appliances in Canada. It currently tracks over 30K unique SKUs, from 150+ independent sources including retailers and OEMs of all sizes. Need a fridge, stove, dishwasher, microwave, oven, TV, soundbar, laptop, camera, lens, unlocked phone, vacuum, printer, toner/ink, etc.? HomeGadgets.ca can definitely help get the very best price! The catalog is available through the website, as well as an MCP server, OpenAPI, desktop applications, with browser extensions and mobile apps coming soon. 8 months after launch, the site is reached via third-party AI chatbots on average 3 times more than organic searchers do. It has been a great learning experience on cataloging for AI shopping agents to say the least!

Background

The short-ish version

My first degree is in Mechanical Engineering. Love understanding the physics, design, processes and transformations associated with machines and their applications. Soon after finishing that degree, I received a Graduate Fellowship from the World Bank that changed my life, so I continued my studies in the US earning a Master of Engineering focused on R&D Management from the Lockheed Martin Engineering Management Program at the University of Colorado Boulder. Then I immigrated to Canada via a Graduate Fellowship from the University of Toronto, and then completed an MBA from York University's Schulich School of Business. The most recent “back to school” moment was finishing the Program on Negotiation and Leadership at Harvard.

My career has moved between helping introduce, build, scale, transform and commercialize new capabilities at existing companies from very big to very small, as well as building my own. I've co-founded four ventures with two exits, and held senior roles at Intangles, Maple Leaf Sports & Entertainment, Upstream Systems, TELUS, Bell Canada, and others.

I started my journey helping automate processes at large oil and gas facilities, working for a system integrator of Honeywell and Allen-Bradley, then managed a nationwide sales organization for a division of what is now Siemens, and from Canada I led international product management and go to market at Husky Injection Moulding Systems.

Since early 2025, I've focused on advisory work supporting venture-backed companies across agentic AI, agentic shopping, quant finance, fintech, and media with strategy, product development, and commercial scaling.

I can conduct business in English, Spanish, and Italian. Two US patent applications, one issued. Based in Toronto. Canadian citizen.
